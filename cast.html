Cast

Zach Rolf
Zach Rolf is a fanatic, passionate, and very tall actor/writer with a deep love for the theater. A 2025 graduate of the American Musical and Dramatic Academy in NYC, he has been seen portraying Buddy the Elf in Elf the Musical and Nigel Bottom in Something Rotten. He has been writing his musical “The King of Scotland” for four years. He wants to wish his parents much thanks for sparking his love of the musical theater.
Iza Junqueira
Iza Junqueira is a Spanish, Brazilian and queer performer that’s thrilled to be able to do what she loves in New York. She’s an AMDA musical theatre graduate where she played roles like the Leading Player in Pippin and studied roles like the Emcee from Cabaret and Beverly Bass from Come from Away. She was also part of the ensemble in Rockaway Theatre. This is her very first time producing and directing an original show, and is beyond thrilled that it’s something she is this passionate about. Thank you to Zach for letting her into this project and thank you to all her family and friends for all the support they give her.
Daniela Morales
Daniela Morales (She/Her/Hers) is a proud Puerto-Rican and graduate of AMDA College of the Performing Arts with a BFA in Musical Theatre. She is also a director, writer, foodie, and deeply passionate for a good decaf coffee. Musicals include “Shrek: The Musical”; Fastrada in “Pippin”; Asaka in “Once On This Island”; “Cabaret”. In a theatre production of “Radium Girls”, she understudied the roles of Irene, Mrs. Fryer, and Miss Wiley. She is grateful to her family, friends, and the team of “The King Of Scotland” for this performance!
Lily Mills
Lily Mills (Witch/Ensemble) is an artist born and raised in Minneapolis, Minnesota. Primarily a musical theatre performer, Lily loves to use art to connect with people and build community. Trained at TU dance, St Paul Conservatory for Performing Artists, and then graduated from The Institute of the Arts Barcelona. Most recently seen in Rockaway Theatre Company’s Man of LaMancha as Antonia.
Leia Shane
Leia Shane is absolutely delighted to be part of such a fun and innovative workshop! As an AMDA New York alum from the integrated program and a born and bred native New Yorker, Leia has been performing since she was a child that looked like Shirley Temple. Unlike Ms. Shirley, she didn't quit acting to marry a rich man in adulthood, which led her here! Previous roles include "Mary Poppins" and "Third Broccoli on the Left" (not the same show). Leia is so grateful to be able to tell this classic story with a sick as hell spin, and thanks her friends, partner and family for the support!
Garrison Glass
Garrison Glass is a New York–based musical theatre performer and proud graduate of the American Musical and Dramatic Academy (AMDA). Emily Nolan
Emily Nolan is a British performer and singer-songwriter originally from Liverpool, England. At the age of 18, she moved to New York City alone to study musical theatre and graduated from the American Music and Dramatic Academy in February 2025. Since then, she has been performing concerts in Manhattan and Brooklyn, singing her original songs, as well as acting in various film and television roles, participating in theatre festivals, and working as a figure model for various art universities around the city. Emily focuses on authentic storytelling both on stage and on set, and loves sharing her music with those who find comfort and identification in shared emotions and experiences.
