Welcome to 11th Century Scotland

From the mind of first time composer/lyricist Zach Rolf comes a brand new take on Shakespeare's infamous ¨Scottish Play¨.

¨The King of Scotland¨ follows similar story beats and characters of ¨The Tradegy of Macbeth¨, but with a punk twist, featuring a completely rock-influenced score.

It evokes groups like The Eagles, artists like Joan Jett, and even some contemporary favorites like Green Day.

It´s a story of ambition, greed, sex and violence. What´s more rock-and-roll than that?

This concert will showcase a selection of songs, previewing the still in-progress musical.
